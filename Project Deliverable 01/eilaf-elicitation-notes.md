# Elicitation Notes – Eilaf
Feature: Follow Artists and Get Concert Alerts (#4)

I looked at apps people already use to keep up with concerts to see what they do well and where they fall short.

## 1. Bandsintown – Follow your favorite artists
![Bandsintown follow artists](bandsintown.png)

- There's a whole page for following artists. You tap the heart on an artist and get alerts and recommendations based on who you follow.
- You can browse by genre or search, and it asks you to pick at least 3 artists. You can also import artists from Spotify, Apple Music, etc.
- This is the closest thing to our feature, and it shows people actually want this.
- Downside: you need an account, and you still have to go to another site to compare or buy tickets.

## 2. Ticketmaster – Artist page
![Ticketmaster artist page](ticketmaster.png)

- There's a small heart icon under the artist name to favorite them, but it's easy to miss.
- For BTS it says "There are no upcoming concerts in United States", so if you want to know when that changes you'd have to keep checking back yourself.
- Some shows are listed "on partner site", so tickets end up spread across different places.
- There's a promoted concert for a totally different artist mixed into the list, so it doesn't feel personalized.

## 3. Spotify – Artist events page
![Spotify events page](spotify.png)

- Spotify has an events page for each artist (I checked KATSEYE) that lists upcoming shows with the date, city, and time. It's simple and easy to read.
- It doesn't show ticket prices, so you have to click through to see how much it costs.
- There's no follow or alert button on this page, just a share icon, so you'd have to remember to come back and check.
- When you're not logged in it just shows "Other locations" instead of shows near you.

## Takeaways for our app
- Users want to follow artists and get notified, but right now it's split across different apps and sites.
- Our app can combine alerts from multiple ticket sources in one place.
- A reminder before tickets go on sale would fix a big problem none of these handle well.
- Alerts should be easy to turn on and off per artist so users don't get spammed.

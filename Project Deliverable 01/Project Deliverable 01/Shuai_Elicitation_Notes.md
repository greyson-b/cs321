Elicitation Materials – Shuai Zheng
1. Ticketmaster
I looked at Ticketmaster to see how users find concerts.
The app shows concerts based on location. It also shows artists, dates, and places.
Our project can use this idea to help users find concerts near them.
2. SeatGeek
I also looked at SeatGeek.
The app shows many events on one page. Users can quickly see ticket prices, dates, and locations.
Our project can use this idea to show concert tickets in one simple view.
What We Learned
Users want to find concerts quickly without clicking too many buttons.
Our Concert Tracker will focus on making concert tickets easy to see and compare.
